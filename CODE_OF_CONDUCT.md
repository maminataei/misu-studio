# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants MUST contribute in a respectful, inclusive, and professional
manner. Constructive disagreement about requirements and evidence is welcome;
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained
personal attacks are not.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers will protect
confidentiality where possible, investigate consistently, avoid conflicts of
interest, and may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently exclude participants.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces, project events, community channels,
and public representation of the project.
